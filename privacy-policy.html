Legal • Privacy

Your information, handled responsibly.

This policy explains what information may be shared with Awadh Classes through this website and how it is used when you make an enquiry.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information we receive

If you submit an enquiry, we may receive information such as the student's name, parent or guardian name, class, board, subjects, mobile number, preferred learning mode and message or requirement details.

2. How we use information

Information shared through an enquiry may be used to respond to your request, understand the student's learning requirements, discuss Home Tuition or Online Classes, and communicate about services you have requested.

3. WhatsApp enquiries

Our enquiry form opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message containing the information you entered. The message is not sent automatically; you review and send it yourself. Once you continue to WhatsApp, the use of that service is also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices.

4. Information sharing

We do not present your enquiry information as public website content. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, provide a requested service, or comply with a legal requirement.

5. Data security

We take reasonable care with information shared through our enquiry process. However, no internet transmission or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Third-party services

This website may use third-party services such as website hosting, web fonts, image hosting and WhatsApp links. Those services may process information according to their own policies and technical practices.

7. Your choices

You may choose what information to include in an enquiry. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive or confidential information through the website or WhatsApp enquiry process.

8. Contact
